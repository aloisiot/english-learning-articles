# Content research

Per-series research and progress tracking — one file per series:

```
research/content/<series-slug>.md
```

Planning a series (picking subjects, researching each one, deciding the
order) happens here **before** any article is drafted. That file, not chat
history, is the source of truth for where a series' research stands.

See [`docs/series.md`](../../docs/series.md) for the format and the
`english-learning-series` skill for the full process.

A series' `description` in `site/content/series.json` is normally the same
sentence(s) as the "Theme & throughline" written here during planning —
copy it over rather than redrafting, so the two stay in sync.

---

Sibling folders under `research/` hold research that isn't about article
content — e.g. [`../dictionary/`](../dictionary/), on adding word lookup to
the site.
